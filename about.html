Elena Marin

Twenty years of selling Victoria’s most distinctive homes.

Born and raised on Vancouver Island, Elena founded Marin & West on a simple conviction: that selling a home should feel as considered and personal as the home itself. She built a practice around fewer clients, deeper relationships, and a standard of marketing that has become the benchmark for luxury real estate in the region.

From oceanfront estates in Oak Bay to heritage residences in Rockland, Elena and her team have guided more than 640 families through their most important moves — quietly, expertly, and with results that speak for themselves.
